Telegram Alert Setup
Receive an instant push notification on your phone the moment your safety tag is tapped.
How it works: When someone taps your Innovat3 NFC tag, our system instantly sends you a Telegram message with the person's name, GPS location, and a Google Maps link — so you or your emergency contacts can respond immediately.
Part 1 — Install Telegram & Create an Account
1
Download Telegram
Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android) on your phone.
Search for "Telegram" and tap Install.
Telegram is completely free to download and use.
2
Create Your Account
Open Telegram → tap Start Messaging → enter your mobile number.
You will receive a 5-digit SMS code — enter it to confirm.
Set a First Name and choose a Username (e.g. @jansmith).
Your username is what you will enter in the Innovat3 registration form.
3
Find Your Username
Tap the ☰ menu (top-left) → Settings.
Your username is shown in grey below your name — it starts with @.
Note it down exactly — you will need it in Step 4.
Part 2 — Link Your Username to Your Innovat3 Tag
4
Start the Innovat3 Bot
In Telegram, tap the search icon (🔍) and search for @Innovat3Bot.
Open the bot and tap Start (or type /start).
This registers your chat ID with our system.
5
Enter Your Username When Registering
On the Innovat3 registration form (Step 2 — Personal Info), enter your Telegram username in the "Telegram Username" field.
Include the @ symbol, e.g. @jansmith.
Your emergency contacts can do the same in the Emergency Contacts section — tick the Telegram toggle and enter their username.
6
Test Your Alerts
After registering, tap your tag with your phone to trigger a test scan.
You should receive a Telegram message within seconds containing the scan location.
If you do not receive a message, re-check that you messaged the bot in Step 4 and your username is spelled correctly.
✅ Tip: Add your emergency contacts' Telegram usernames in the registration form so they also receive instant alerts when your tag is scanned — not just you.
⚠️ Important: You must send at least one message to @Innovat3Bot before alerts will work. If you only enter your username without messaging the bot, the system cannot send you messages.
Troubleshooting
?
I'm not receiving messages
Open Telegram → search @Innovat3Bot → tap Start. Then email support@innovat3.co.za with your tag ID and we will manually link your account.
?
I changed my Telegram username
Log in to your Innovat3 account and update your Telegram username, or email support with your tag ID and new username.
?
I prefer WhatsApp over Telegram
No problem — see the separate WhatsApp (CallMeBot) Setup Guide included with your tag. You can use WhatsApp instead of or alongside Telegram.
