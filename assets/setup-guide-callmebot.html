WhatsApp Alert Setup
Receive an instant WhatsApp message when your safety tag is tapped — no Telegram required.
How it works: CallMeBot is a free service that lets apps send WhatsApp messages to your number. You activate it once by sending a single WhatsApp message — then every time your Innovat3 tag is tapped, you receive a WhatsApp alert with the name, GPS location, and a Google Maps link.
Part 1 — Activate CallMeBot (One-Time Setup — 2 Minutes)
1
Add the CallMeBot Number to Your Contacts
Save the following number in your phone contacts under any name (e.g. "CallMeBot"):
WhatsApp number: +34 644 59 72 93
This is the official CallMeBot WhatsApp service number. You must save it as a contact before WhatsApp will allow you to message it.
2
Send the Activation Message
Open WhatsApp, find the CallMeBot contact you just saved, and send exactly this message:
I allow callmebot to send me messages
Send it as a WhatsApp chat message — not SMS. Within a few seconds you will receive a reply containing your personal API key.
3
Copy Your API Key
CallMeBot will reply with a message like:
Your phone is now connected to CallMeBot. Your API key is: 1234567

The number at the end (e.g. 1234567) is your personal API key. Copy it and keep it safe — you will need it in the next step.
My API Key: ___________________________
(write it here for safe-keeping)
Part 2 — Enter Your Key in the Innovat3 Registration Form
4
Open the Registration Form
Go to innovat3.co.za/register on your phone or computer.
Complete your personal details as normal.
5
Paste Your API Key
In Step 2 — Personal Info, scroll to the field labelled "WhatsApp Alerts — CallMeBot API Key".
Paste the API key you received in Step 3.
Your registered phone number is automatically used — no need to enter it again.
6
Add Your Emergency Contacts (Optional)
In the Emergency Contacts section, for each contact:
1. Enter their name and phone number.
2. Tick the WhatsApp toggle.
3. A new field appears — "CallMeBot API Key".
4. Your contact must follow Steps 1–3 above on their own phone to get their key, then share it with you to enter here.

This allows your emergency contacts to also receive a WhatsApp alert when your tag is scanned.
✅ Tip: You can use both WhatsApp AND Telegram at the same time — just fill in both fields. Alerts will be sent to both apps simultaneously.
⚠️ Important: Your API key is personal — it is linked to your WhatsApp number and cannot be shared or reused on a different number. Each person (including emergency contacts) needs their own key.

Troubleshooting
?
I never received a reply from CallMeBot
Make sure you saved +34 644 59 72 93 as a contact first. WhatsApp blocks messages to unsaved numbers. Try deleting and re-adding the contact, then send the activation message again.
?
My tag was scanned but I got no WhatsApp message
Confirm your API key is entered correctly in the registration form (no spaces or extra characters). You can email support@innovat3.co.za with your tag ID and we will verify the key on file.
?
I changed my phone number
Your CallMeBot API key is linked to your old number. You must repeat Steps 1–3 with your new number to get a new key, then update it in your Innovat3 profile by emailing support.
?
I prefer Telegram
See the separate Telegram Setup Guide included with your tag. Both systems work the same way once configured — use whichever app you prefer, or both.
