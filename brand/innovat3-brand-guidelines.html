Do & Don't
✓ DO
Use on dark (#0A0A0A) or very dark backgrounds
Maintain clear space equal to the logo height on all sides
Use SVG files for all digital applications
Apply orange glow: drop-shadow(0 0 12px rgba(249,115,22,0.55))
✗ DON'T
Do not recolour the logo without brand approval
Do not stretch or distort proportions
Do not place on mid-grey or low-contrast backgrounds
Do not use the "3" in any colour other than white or orange

Brand Voice & Copy — Page 6
Tone & Voice
Brand Voice
Every piece of Innovat3 communication must feel urgent, trustworthy, and direct. Lives depend on this product — the copy must reflect that.
Canva Brand Voice Field — paste this exactly

Bold, direct, urgent. We build NFC emergency tags that send GPS alerts in under 3 seconds — no app needed. Speak to first responders, families, and pet owners: clear, life-saving, trustworthy. No fluff. Use short sentences. Prioritise speed and reliability. Headlines in Orbitron. Body in Exo 2. Always: "Made in South Africa."

Voice Pillars
Short sentences. Always.
✓ "No app. No battery. Instant access."
✗ "Our innovative solution requires no application installation and functions without any battery power, providing immediate access to information."
Lead with speed & outcome.
✓ "In under 3 seconds, your family knows where you are."
✗ "After someone scans your tag, a process is initiated that sends a notification..."
Always mention origin.
Every final design should include "Made in South Africa." — in Exo 2, uppercase, muted colour.
Numbers sell it.
Use specific numbers wherever possible: 3 seconds · R49/mo · 10 minutes setup · Patent ZA 2026/1539553.
